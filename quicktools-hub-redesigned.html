About QuickTools Hub

QuickTools Hub is a collection of practical online utilities designed to make everyday digital tasks easier. Use the QR code generator to share links and contact details, resize images for web use, create random passwords, count words, convert text to Base64, generate URL slugs and pick random numbers.

Our goal is to keep the tools straightforward, accessible and easy to use on desktop and mobile devices. Many operations run directly in your browser, so the information you enter stays on your device for those tools.

How to use QuickTools Hub

  1. Choose a tool from the tool cards or search for it by name.
  2. Enter the information requested by that tool.
  3. Select the action button to generate, convert, scan or process your result.
  4. Copy or download your result when the tool provides that option.

For the QR scanner, allow camera permission when prompted. For image processing, select an image from your device. Always check important results before sharing or publishing them.
